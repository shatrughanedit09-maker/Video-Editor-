Scene 01 — About
Shahjahanpur, IN

Shatrughan Singhania is a freelance video editor and VFX editor with 3+ years spent behind the timeline — cutting cinematic edits, VFX and campaign content for creators, brands and real estate businesses that span fashion, travel, hospitality and events, including a fashion creator with an audience north of 80K.

It started with travel footage — a fascination with wide compositions, natural light, and footage that feels like a memory rather than a video. That eye for a frame now drives everything he cuts: pacing, storytelling, sound design, colour, motion and VFX. Post-production is the strongest skill; shooting is available when a client specifically needs it.

He also runs Shahjahanpur Adswala, a content and social media agency — so brand thinking isn't bolted on after the edit, it's part of the first cut. The result is work that looks cinematic and performs commercially.

“Retention first, aesthetics always. A beautiful edit nobody finishes is just a beautiful waste of footage.”
